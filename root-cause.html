ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS

Understanding Why an Incident Occurred

Five Oceans Marine provides independent root cause analysis following marine incidents, equipment failures, cargo damage and other operational events.

Our investigations examine the available evidence, sequence of events, operating conditions and relevant contributing factors to develop a clear understanding of how and why an incident occurred.

The analysis considers both immediate causes and underlying contributing factors, helping clients understand weaknesses in procedures, equipment, communication or operational practices.

Findings are presented in a structured and independent report, supported by relevant evidence, observations and photographs where appropriate.

Incident Analysis

Examination of marine incidents and operational events to establish the sequence of events and identify relevant contributing factors.

Failure Analysis

Investigation of equipment, machinery and operational failures to identify potential causes and contributing circumstances.

Causal Factors

Identification and assessment of factors that may have contributed to an incident, failure or damage event.

Sequence of Events

Reconstruction of the available sequence of events using observations, documentation, photographs and other relevant information.

Operational Review

Review of relevant operational procedures, conditions and practices associated with the incident or failure.

Evidence Assessment

Independent assessment of available physical, photographic and documentary evidence relevant to the investigation.

Corrective Actions

Identification of potential areas for improvement based on the findings and contributing factors identified during the investigation.

Investigation Reporting

Preparation of a structured independent report presenting observations, analysis, findings and supporting evidence where required.
