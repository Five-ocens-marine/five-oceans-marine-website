Independent Heavy Lift Supervision

Five Oceans Marine provides independent supervision of heavy lift and project cargo operations for vessel owners, operators, charterers, cargo owners and other maritime stakeholders.

Our surveyors monitor the handling of heavy and oversized cargo throughout the operation, from preparation and lifting through loading, positioning and completion.

Particular attention is given to cargo condition, lifting arrangements, handling procedures, stowage, securing and visible operational conditions that may require attention.

Following completion of the operation, detailed observations and photographs can be recorded to provide clients with an independent report of the heavy lift operation.

Heavy Lift Operations

Independent supervision of heavy lift operations, including monitoring lifting, handling and cargo movement throughout the operation.

Oversized Cargo

Supervision of handling operations involving oversized, heavy or project cargo requiring specialised operational arrangements.

Lifting Arrangements

Observation of visible lifting arrangements, including lifting points, slings, shackles and other accessible lifting components.

Cargo Handling

Monitoring of cargo handling procedures and identification of visible conditions that may affect the safe and efficient movement of cargo.

Cargo Condition

Monitoring of cargo condition before, during and after handling, including identification of visible damage, deterioration or other concerns.

Stowage

Observation of cargo positioning and stowage arrangements to identify visible conditions requiring attention.

Securing & Lashing

Observation of visible cargo securing and lashing arrangements to identify conditions requiring corrective attention.

Operational Reporting

Detailed independent reporting documenting heavy lift activities, cargo condition, observations and photographs where required.
