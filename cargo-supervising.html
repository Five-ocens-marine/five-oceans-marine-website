Independent Cargo Supervising Services

Five Oceans Marine provides independent cargo supervising services to ensure cargo operations are carried out safely, efficiently, and in accordance with international maritime standards.

Our surveyors supervise every stage of cargo handling, from the beginning of loading or discharging until completion. We monitor cargo condition, handling procedures, stowage, securing arrangements, and compliance with operational requirements.

During the operation, we maintain close communication with the vessel's crew, terminal personnel, stevedores, charterers, and cargo owners to ensure any issues are identified and addressed immediately.

Following completion of the operation, we provide a detailed report documenting our observations, including photographs where required, giving clients with an accurate and independent record of the cargo operation.

Cargo Loading

Independent supervision of cargo loading operations, including monitoring cargo handling, condition, stowage and compliance with operational requirements.

Cargo Discharging

Supervision of cargo discharging operations to ensure cargo is handled safely and efficiently while identifying any issues during the operation.

Cargo Condition

Monitoring of cargo condition during loading and discharging, including identification of visible damage, deterioration or other concerns.

Reporting

Detailed independent reporting of cargo operations, including observations and photographs where required, providing clients with an accurate record of the operation.
