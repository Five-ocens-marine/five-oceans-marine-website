NAVIGATION ASSESSMENT

Independent Assessment of Navigation Practices

Five Oceans Marine provides independent navigation assessment services for vessel owners and operators seeking an additional review of bridge operations, navigational arrangements and working practices.

The assessment focuses on accessible navigation areas, equipment arrangements, operational practices and available documentation within the agreed scope of the assignment.

The objective is to identify observable conditions, practices or areas that may benefit from corrective action, further review or operational improvement.

Findings can be supported with structured observations, photographs and a clear technical report.

Bridge Arrangement

Review of accessible bridge arrangements and equipment positioning to identify visible conditions that may require attention.

Navigation Equipment

Visual assessment of accessible navigational equipment and associated arrangements within the agreed inspection scope.

Navigation Practices

Review of observable navigation practices and working arrangements to identify areas where additional attention may be beneficial.

Bridge Procedures

Review of available bridge procedures and operational arrangements relevant to the agreed scope of the assessment.

Passage Planning

Review of available passage-planning arrangements and documentation to identify observable gaps or areas requiring further attention.

Navigation Safety

Assessment of accessible navigation safety arrangements and observable practices that may require corrective attention.

Documentation Review

Review of relevant navigation records, procedures and available documentation within the agreed scope of the assignment.

Assessment Report

Structured reporting of observations, findings and identified areas requiring attention to support vessel operators in their ongoing navigation and safety management.
