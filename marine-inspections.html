MARINE INSPECTIONS

Practical and Independent Inspection Services

Five Oceans Marine provides independent marine inspections for vessel owners, operators, charterers, insurers and other maritime stakeholders.

Inspections are focused on identifying visible defects, operational concerns and conditions that may require further attention.

Vessel Inspection

General inspection of accessible areas of the vessel and identification of visible deficiencies.

Equipment Inspection

Review of accessible equipment and identification of visible damage, deterioration or operational concerns.

Operational Inspection

Independent attendance during marine operations to observe conditions and operational practices.

Inspection Reporting

Clear and structured reporting of observations, findings and areas requiring attention.
