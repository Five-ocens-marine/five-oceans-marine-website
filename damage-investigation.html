DAMAGE INVESTIGATION

Independent Marine Damage Investigation

Five Oceans Marine provides independent damage investigation services following incidents involving vessels, cargo, equipment and marine operations.

Our surveyors attend the relevant location and assess the available evidence to establish the nature and extent of damage and document the circumstances surrounding the incident.

Investigations may include inspection of damaged cargo, vessel structures, machinery, equipment and other accessible areas relevant to the incident.

Findings are documented in a clear and independent report, supported by photographs and relevant observations where required.

Damage Assessment

Assessment and documentation of visible damage to vessels, cargo, equipment and other marine assets following an incident.

Cargo Damage

Investigation of damaged cargo to document its condition, location, extent of damage and other relevant observations.

Vessel Damage

Inspection and documentation of accessible vessel structures, equipment and areas affected by collision, contact or other incidents.

Equipment Damage

Examination of damaged marine equipment and machinery to document visible conditions and relevant evidence.

Incident Evidence

Collection and documentation of available observations, photographs and relevant information associated with the incident.

Cause Assessment

Assessment of available evidence to identify potential contributing factors and circumstances associated with the reported damage.

Photographic Documentation

Detailed photographic documentation of relevant damage, affected areas and visible conditions identified during the investigation.

Investigation Reporting

Preparation of an independent investigation report presenting observations, findings and supporting photographic evidence where required.
