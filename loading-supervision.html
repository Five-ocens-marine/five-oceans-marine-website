LOADING SUPERVISION

Independent Cargo Loading Supervision

Five Oceans Marine provides independent supervision of cargo loading operations for vessel owners, operators, charterers, cargo owners, insurers and other maritime stakeholders.

Our surveyors supervise every stage of cargo handling, from the beginning of loading until completion. We monitor cargo condition, handling procedures, stowage, securing arrangements and compliance with operational requirements.

During the operation, we maintain close communication with the vessel's crew, terminal personnel, stevedores, charterers and cargo owners to ensure any issues are identified and addressed immediately.

Following completion of the operation, we provide a detailed report documenting our observations, including photographs where required, giving clients an accurate and independent record of the cargo operation.

Cargo Loading

Independent supervision of cargo loading operations, including monitoring cargo handling, condition, stowage and compliance with operational requirements.

Cargo Condition

Monitoring of cargo condition during loading, including identification of visible damage, deterioration or other concerns.

Cargo Handling

Observation of cargo handling procedures and identification of visible issues that may affect the safe and efficient loading of cargo.

Cargo Stowage

Monitoring of cargo stowage arrangements and identification of visible conditions requiring attention during the loading operation.

Operational Safety

Observation of visible safety-related conditions during loading and identification of issues that may require corrective action.

Loading Progress

Monitoring of the loading operation from commencement through completion, with observations recorded throughout the operation.

Communication

Close communication with the vessel's crew, terminal personnel, stevedores, charterers and cargo interests throughout the operation.

Reporting

Detailed independent reporting of cargo operations, including observations and photographs where required, providing clients with an accurate record of the operation.
