LASHING VERIFICATION

Independent Cargo Securing Verification

Five Oceans Marine provides independent verification of cargo securing and lashing arrangements for vessel owners, operators, charterers, cargo owners and other maritime stakeholders.

Our surveyors inspect and monitor accessible cargo securing arrangements during loading and prior to completion of the operation. Particular attention is given to the condition and arrangement of visible lashings, securing points and cargo supports.

The purpose of the verification is to identify visible deficiencies or conditions that may require attention before the vessel proceeds with the cargo operation.

Following completion, we can provide an independent report documenting the observed securing arrangements, relevant findings and photographs where required.

Lashing Inspection

Inspection of accessible lashing arrangements and identification of visible conditions that may require attention.

Cargo Securing

Verification of visible cargo securing arrangements to support safe and controlled transportation of break bulk and project cargo.

Securing Points

Observation of accessible securing points, fittings and connection arrangements used to secure cargo.

Lashing Equipment

Visual inspection of accessible lashing equipment, including chains, wires, straps, turnbuckles and other securing components.

Cargo Supports

Observation of visible cargo supports, dunnage and other arrangements used to maintain cargo position during transportation.

Heavy & Project Cargo

Lashing verification for heavy, oversized and project cargo requiring specialised securing arrangements.

Pre-Departure Verification

Verification of visible securing arrangements prior to completion of cargo operations and vessel departure.

Reporting

Independent reporting of observed lashing and securing arrangements, including photographs and relevant observations where required.
