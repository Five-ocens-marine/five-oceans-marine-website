CONDITION ASSESSMENT

Independent Vessel Condition Assessment

Five Oceans Marine provides independent condition surveys for vessels, owners, operators, charterers, insurers and other maritime stakeholders.

The survey provides an objective assessment of the vessel's physical condition and identifies areas requiring attention, maintenance or further investigation.

Vessel Condition

Assessment of the general condition of the vessel, including accommodation, deck areas, machinery spaces and visible structural components.

Equipment

Inspection of visible operational equipment and identification of defects, deterioration or abnormal conditions.

Safety

Review of visible safety-related conditions and identification of issues that may require corrective action.

Reporting

Clear photographic-free technical reporting based on observations made during the survey.
