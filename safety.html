SAFETY INSPECTIONS

Independent Marine Safety Inspections

Five Oceans Marine provides independent safety inspection services for vessels, marine operations and associated equipment.

Our inspections focus on accessible safety-related arrangements, equipment and operational conditions. The scope can be tailored to the vessel, operation and requirements of the client.

Inspections can help identify visible deficiencies, damaged equipment, unsafe conditions and other areas that may require corrective attention.

Findings are documented in a clear and independent report, supported by photographs and observations where required.

Vessel Safety Inspection

Inspection of accessible vessel areas and safety-related arrangements to identify visible conditions requiring attention.

Safety Equipment

Visual inspection of accessible safety equipment and associated arrangements within the agreed scope of the assignment.

Emergency Equipment

Observation of accessible emergency equipment, arrangements and visible conditions relevant to vessel safety.

Deck Safety

Inspection of accessible deck areas, walkways, working spaces and other visible conditions affecting safe operations.

Fire Safety

Visual inspection of accessible fire safety equipment and arrangements within the agreed inspection scope.

Personal Safety

Observation of visible conditions and arrangements relevant to personnel safety during marine operations.

Deficiency Identification

Identification and documentation of visible deficiencies, damaged equipment or conditions requiring further attention.

Safety Reporting

Structured reporting of inspection findings, observations and supporting photographic evidence where required.
