COMPLIANCE SUPPORT

Practical Support for Marine Compliance

Five Oceans Marine provides independent compliance support for vessel owners, operators and maritime stakeholders.

Our services help clients identify visible compliance concerns, review relevant documentation and understand areas that may require further attention.

The scope of each assignment can be adapted to the vessel, operation, applicable requirements and specific needs of the client.

Findings can be documented through structured reports, observations and photographic evidence to provide a clear record of the inspection or review.

Compliance Inspections

Independent inspection support to identify visible conditions, practices or arrangements that may require attention in relation to applicable requirements.

Regulatory Support

Practical support in reviewing relevant maritime requirements and identifying areas that may require further assessment or corrective action.

Documentation Review

Review of relevant vessel documents, records and available information within the agreed scope of the assignment.

Compliance Assessment

Assessment of accessible vessel and operational conditions to identify visible areas requiring further attention.

Deficiency Identification

Identification and documentation of visible deficiencies, non-conformities or conditions that may require corrective attention.

Corrective Action Support

Independent observations and practical recommendations to assist operators in addressing identified areas of concern.

Compliance Documentation

Structured documentation of inspection findings, observations, photographs and relevant supporting information.

Compliance Reporting

Professional reporting providing a clear and independent record of findings, observations and areas requiring further review.
